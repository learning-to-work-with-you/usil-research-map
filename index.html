Learning to Work with You

How should an agent learn to work with you?

This map annotates the research behind User-Specific Interaction Learning (USIL): work on learning about individual users, and work on when agents should ask, act, confirm, or defer. Each paper is labeled by what it learns, whether it learns from one person's evidence, and which interaction decisions it affects.

How to read the labels. Labels describe what each paper reports, not what its methods could support. Papers marked Full text were annotated from the full paper with quotes checked against the text; the fourteen in Closest predecessors were rated by hand. Other labels come from the catalog and may change. "Not established" means the paper does not say, not that the capability is absent.
